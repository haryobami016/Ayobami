Work

One live project in detail, and two others in brief.

Pearl by Mary

Online store for a handmade jewellery brand in Lagos

  • Role: Designer and builder
  • Tools: Claude, Vercel, GitHub
  • Status: Live, early stage
  • marypearl.vercel.app

The problem

Mary sold only through Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers told her they did not know how to order, and sales stayed low without a site of her own. Her buyers are mostly students and working people, and most of them shop on a phone.

What I built

  • Shop by category, with new arrivals showing prices and ratings
  • Custom order requests, customer reviews and newsletter sign-up
  • Payment options: Paystack, Flutterwave and bank transfer
  • A mobile-first layout that is easy to browse one-handed

A decision I changed

My first design was dark and luxurious. I ran a look poll on WhatsApp and Instagram with 68 participants, and combined the result with Mary's own taste. The site moved to a warm blush palette.

What I would do differently

Run the poll before the first design, and plan the payment setup from day one. I launched in stages to get something useful online within a small budget and a short timeline.
